# Future ideas

## How much earlier could an agent have confirmed the same hit?

Start RENIN from the same dated opening inventory as the historical project. Use the first **returned assay** meeting a prespecified potency threshold (or confirming the same compound) as the finish. The archival record gives the date of the experimental result. The time the chemists personally spent on a compound is unknown.

**First experiment.** Let the player choose up to *B* compounds per CRO submission. Assume results return together seven calendar days after submission. Fix the choices at dispatch and keep the assays hidden until the batch returns. Score the hit on its return date. For the simplest comparison, send one batch at a time and assume compounds are ready to dispatch. Nine sequential batches then take nine weeks of turnaround, regardless of whether each contains one compound or four. Synthesis still consumes operations and cash. Its elapsed duration is unspecified.

Replay batch capacities of 1, 2, 4 and 8 from the same inventory, with the same cash and paired FEP error draws. Compare time to confirmed hit, synthesis steps, compounds assayed and success rate. Larger batches can test more ideas per week but must commit to more compounds before the next assay result. Later, vary turnaround, preparation lead times and whether multiple submissions can overlap.

**Historical comparison.** For a dated campaign, subtract the modeled elapsed time from the observed interval between the common opening date and the historical qualifying measurement. A positive value means earlier confirmation under the stated assumptions. Label the initial result as a turnaround-only scenario. A claim about total calendar acceleration also needs a synthesis schedule. For undated patents, report batches, operations, and modeled weeks within the scenario.

**What exists today.** Manual play reveals a result on each make action. Current strategy replays shortlist two candidates for RENIN and four for ABL1 and S1P1, then execute the selections one at a time. Archived RENIN forward runs release results at batch completion and previously compared batch sizes 1, 2, 4 and 8. None of these rules assigns a CRO return date.
