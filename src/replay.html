Replay assumptions

Both paths start with the same 15 assayed compounds and retained intermediates. An ideal FEP query reveals the measured affinity of a one-move neighbour of a made reference (OpenFE LoMap score ≥ 0.90). Each preparation operation costs one step. Shared reactions in a batch are paid once. The chart places compounds at their recorded experimental dates.

Hindsight supplies the winner in advance. All 47 shortest paths require five new compounds. Of those, 23 cost 17 synthesis steps and 24 cost 24 steps. The forward rule queries available neighbours and makes two at a time, ranking them by predicted affinity rank plus the fraction of new neighbours they unlock. Its rule was selected retrospectively from four rules and four batch sizes. Structures, candidate availability and ideal FEP accuracy are assumptions of this replay.
